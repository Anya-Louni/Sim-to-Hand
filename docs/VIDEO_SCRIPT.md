# 60-second demo script

Record https://sim-to-hand.vercel.app at 1280x720. Read the voice lines at a calm pace. Check every number against the live pages before recording.

| Time | Screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:08 | App loaded. Click "3-cube tower", turn Chaos on, press Run. | "This is an AI agent for a robot arm. I give it a task in plain English." |
| 0:08 to 0:20 | Plan card streams in, arm starts moving. | "A language model writes a plan from five skills. The physics simulation runs it in the browser, with inverse kinematics doing the control." |
| 0:20 to 0:32 | Chaos card: grasp slips. Replan card appears. | "Chaos mode makes the grasp slip. The robot feels it through cheap fingertip and force sensors, and the agent replans from the new state." |
| 0:32 to 0:40 | Verify card with three columns. | "Every attempt gets three checks: the simulator, a vision model looking at a camera image, and the robot's own sensors." |
| 0:40 to 0:52 | Open /benchmark, play one "Can you tell?" round, show the reveal. | "Why sensors? In this study, free vision models judging millimeter contact were close to a coin flip, and called about half the failures a success. Cheap sensors were right far more often." |
| 0:52 to 1:00 | Leaderboard push column, then the GitHub page. | "Sensors are not perfect either: they miss sideways pushes. Everything is open: code, data and every model answer." |
