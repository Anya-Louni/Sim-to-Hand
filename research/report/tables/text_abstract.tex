On 400 samples the best free VLM we could run at scale reached 55\% balanced accuracy (95\% CI [50, 60]) with a 54\% false-success rate; a logistic regression on the degraded sensors reached 89\% ([85, 92]) with 11\%. Sensors fail where they cannot observe the contact (push, 50\%) and do not transfer across tasks. Some VLMs still call a failure a success at gaps of several millimeters. Free-tier quotas limited coverage for several models; we report it throughout.
